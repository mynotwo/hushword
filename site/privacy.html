Privacy, in plain language.

Effective for Hushword v{{VERSION}}.

Local data

Hushword stores your preferences, personal dictionary, correction memory, recent transcript history, and private performance timings in your Mac's Application Support directory. Hushword does not operate a telemetry or advertising service.

Voice recognition

The bundled Whisper model processes recordings on your Mac. With Keep audio on this Mac on, which is the default, Apple Speech Recognition uses only Apple's on-device models. If you turn that setting off, Apple Speech Recognition may send audio to Apple's servers, and Apple's privacy terms apply to that data.

No AI rewrite service

Hushword does not send transcripts to any language model or AI provider.

Automatic paste

Accessibility permission is used only to return focus to your chosen app and insert text. If permission is unavailable, Hushword copies the transcript to the clipboard instead.

Model download and updates

Hushword connects to the configured download host to retrieve the verified Whisper model and signed application updates. The model is accepted only when its byte count and SHA-256 checksum match the pinned release asset.
