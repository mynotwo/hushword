Privacy, in plain language.

Effective for Hushword v{{VERSION}}.

Local data

Hushword stores your preferences, personal dictionary, correction memory, recent transcript history, and private performance timings in your Mac's Application Support directory. Hushword does not operate a telemetry or advertising service.

Voice recognition

The bundled Whisper model processes recordings on your Mac. Apple Speech Recognition may process audio using Apple's service when on-device recognition is unavailable or not required. Apple's handling of that data is governed by Apple's privacy terms.

Optional Agent rewrite

Agent rewrite is disabled when no compatible Agent is selected or installed. If you use it, Hushword sends the selected transcript to the Claude, Codex, or Coco command-line provider already installed and signed in on your Mac. That provider's privacy terms apply.

Automatic paste

Accessibility permission is used only to return focus to your chosen app and insert or safely replace text. If permission is unavailable, Hushword copies the transcript to the clipboard instead.

Model download and updates

Hushword connects to the configured download host to retrieve the verified Whisper model and signed application updates. The model is accepted only when its byte count and SHA-256 checksum match the pinned release asset.
